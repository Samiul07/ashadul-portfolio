Brutalist web design is making a massive comeback, especially in the developer tool and premium SaaS spaces. Modern brutalism isn’t about making websites ugly; it’s about **confidence, speed, and high information density**.

## Key Design Pillars

1. **Achromatic Foundations**: Pure black backgrounds (`#030303`) with absolute white text (`#FFFFFF`).
2. **Aggressive Accent Colors**: Using primary red (`#FF2200`) as a singular, laser-focused signifier for primary actions and alerts.
3. **Hairline Layout Borders**: Thin `1px` borders with low opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) instead of heavy shadows.
